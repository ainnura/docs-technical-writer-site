# Installation Guide

You can access Taskify directly from your browser — no installation required.  
However, for a smoother experience, Taskify can also be installed as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**.

### Desktop Installation
1. Open Taskify in your browser (e.g., Google Chrome).  
2. Click the **Install Taskify** icon in the address bar.  
3. Taskify will appear on your desktop and can be launched like a native app.

### Mobile Installation
1. Open Taskify in your mobile browser.  
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.  
3. Taskify will appear on your home screen like a regular app.
