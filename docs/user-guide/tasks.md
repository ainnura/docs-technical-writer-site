# Managing Tasks in Taskify

Taskify makes it easy to create, assign, and track tasks across your team.

### Creating a New Task
- Click **+ New Task** from your dashboard.  
- Fill in task details such as title, description, and due date.  
- Assign it to a team member.

### Tracking Progress
Use the **Kanban Board** to visualize task progress:
- **To Do**: tasks that haven’t been started.  
- **In Progress**: tasks currently being worked on.  
- **Done**: completed tasks.

### Productivity Tips
Use filters and search to quickly find tasks by assignee, status, or due date.
